# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

Northstar Manufacturing protects customer, employee, and operational information throughout its lifecycle. This policy defines the minimum controls required for company systems and data.

## Access control

Access must follow the principle of least privilege. Managers review privileged access every quarter, and system owners remove access within one business day after an employee changes roles or leaves the company.

## Incident reporting

Employees must report suspected security incidents to the Security Operations team immediately. The incident commander records the affected systems, containment actions, business impact, and recovery decision in the incident register.

## Data retention

Business records are retained according to the corporate retention schedule. Records subject to a legal hold must not be altered or deleted until Legal releases the hold.
